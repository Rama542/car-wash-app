We collect your name, mobile number, email (if you sign in with Google), car details, number plate, parking address and booking history. We use them only to provide and improve the service: to show your booking to the assigned washer, to contact you about it, and to keep records.

  • Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; data is stored in Google Cloud (Mumbai region).
  • Online payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI PIN details.
  • Your address and phone number are shown to a washer only after they accept your booking.
  • We do not sell your data. To see or delete your data, email [support@yourdomain.in]; we respond within [7] days.
